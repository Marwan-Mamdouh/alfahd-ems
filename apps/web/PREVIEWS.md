# Demo previews

These direct URLs are temporary, demo-only views outside ordinary navigation:

- `/employees-preview`
- `/attendance-preview`
- `/warehouses-preview`
- `/inventory-preview`
- `/routers-preview`

They use mock data, are not connected to the backend, and do not persist changes: refreshing resets their local state. They do not represent completed Phase 2 functionality. The regular `/employees` page remains API-backed; attendance, warehouses, inventory, and routers remain marked as coming soon in navigation.
